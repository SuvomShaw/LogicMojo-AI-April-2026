Title: Laptop and loss reporting

## Standard laptops

Engineering laptops are MacBook machines and are refreshed every 36 months. Finance laptops run Windows and are refreshed every 36 months.

## Loss or theft

A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours of the employee noticing the loss. Send the report to security@harbordesk.example and open a ticket in the queue IT-LOST.
