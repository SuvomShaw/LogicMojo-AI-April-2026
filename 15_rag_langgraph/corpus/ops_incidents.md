Title: Incident severity clocks

## Sev-1

A Sev-1 incident is a customer-facing outage. The on-call engineer acknowledges the page within 15 minutes and opens the channel #incidents. The Sev-1 commander is the on-call engineering manager.

## Sev-2

A Sev-2 incident is a degraded feature that is not a full outage. The on-call engineer acknowledges the page within 60 minutes.
