Title: Travel expense policy

## Meal allowances

On a domestic trip, the meal allowance is INR 1500 per person per day. On an international trip, the meal allowance is USD 40 per person per day. A receipt is required for every meal claim.

## Policy codes

EXP-MEAL-DOM authorises the domestic meal allowance.
EXP-MEAL-INTL authorises the international meal allowance.
EXP-APPR-MGR requires line-manager approval before reimbursement when the claim is above INR 10000.
EXP-APPR-FIN requires Finance approval before the purchase when the amount is above INR 50000.
