Title: Annual leave policy

## Entitlement

A full-time HarborDesk employee receives 18 working days of annual leave in each calendar year. Sick leave is a separate balance of 10 working days. At HarborDesk, the abbreviation PTO means annual leave.

## Carry forward and approval

At most 5 unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Annual leave longer than 3 consecutive working days needs written approval from the line manager before the leave starts.

## Joiners

An employee who joins during the year accrues 1.5 annual-leave days for each completed month in that joining year.
